## About

**Sports Info** brings sports data into a single widget and lets you choose a sport, competition and favorite team. The layout adapts automatically to the widget size and its individual sections.

## Supported sports

- ⚽ football
- 🏒 hockey
- 🏀 basketball
- 🥅 floorball
- ⚾ baseball

## Main features

- 🔴 live match status
- ⏭️ upcoming games and ⏮️ recent results
- 📊 standings and team form
- 🎯 sport, competition and favorite-team selection
- 📱 Small / Medium / Large widgets
- 📐 automatic and manual alignment
- 👁️ widget preview directly from settings
- 🌍 Czech, English, German and Spanish
- 🔄 in-app updates through Scriptable
