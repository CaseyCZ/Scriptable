## What it does

**Breakout Deluxe** brings classic brick-breaking gameplay to iPhone. Bounce the ball, clear rows of bricks and advance to harder levels.

## Key features

- 🧱 Colorful bricks and progressively harder levels.
- 👆 Move the paddle by dragging your finger.
- ❤️ Three lives, scoring and a locally saved high score.
- 🎯 Easy, Normal and Hard difficulty settings.
- 🎨 Space, Ocean and Neon themes.
- 🔊 Optional sound, pause and restart controls.
- 🔄 Automatic update checks and a manual check in Settings.

## Getting started

1. Install **Breakout Deluxe** in Scriptable and open it.
2. Tap **Start** or the game board to launch the ball.
3. Drag left or right to keep the ball in play.
4. Clear all bricks to progress and try to beat your personal best.

**Example:** Try Easy to learn the controls, then choose Hard for a faster game with a smaller paddle.

## Controls and difficulty

Keep the paddle beneath the ball. Higher difficulties increase the challenge and reduce paddle width. Use **Pause** to stop play temporarily.

## Updates

Updates are checked automatically at launch. ⚙️ Settings shows the app version, check status and a **Check for updates** button. Available updates are offered when the game is launched again; the previous script is backed up before replacement. Playing offline is unaffected.

## Good to know

Gameplay works offline; an internet connection is only needed to check for and download updates. High scores and settings are stored locally. This is a Scriptable game, not a Home Screen widget.

**Installation:** Open [Breakout Deluxe.scriptable](./Breakout%20Deluxe.scriptable) with Scriptable. If iOS shows it as plain text, download the [JavaScript file](./Breakout%20Deluxe.js) and save it under **iCloud Drive → Scriptable**.
